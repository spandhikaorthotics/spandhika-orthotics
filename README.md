## About Spandhika Orthotics

**Spandhika Orthotics Private Limited** is a MedTech startup based in Himachal Pradesh, India, building continuous, affordable foot-health monitoring for a country where clinical-grade gait and pressure diagnostics have historically been confined to a handful of tertiary hospitals.

Our flagship product, **SAARTHI** (internally developed as **IOLMS** — Intelligent Orthotic & Leg Monitoring System), is a smart insole embedded with a 32-node pressure sensor array and a 9-axis motion sensor. It maps plantar pressure and gait in real time, at home, in regular footwear — closing the gap between infrequent clinic visits and the daily reality of how a patient actually walks.

We work at the intersection of diabetic foot care, post-surgical rehabilitation, and elderly fall prevention — three of the largest and most under-monitored patient populations in Indian healthcare.

## SAARTHI / IOLMS — The Product

> A continuous, home-use smart insole system that measures plantar pressure and gait — not just at a single clinic visit, but throughout daily life.

### Hardware Specifications

| Component | Specification | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Pressure Sensor Array | 32 FSR sensors (4×8 matrix), 0–1,000 kPa range | Full plantar surface mapping; detects high-risk pressure zones |
| Motion Sensor (IMU) | 9-axis (accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer) | Derives gait parameters including dorsiflexion and foot progression angle |
| Microcontroller | Dual-core RISC-V, BLE 5.0 + Wi-Fi | On-device processing and wireless sync |
| Battery | 1,000 mAh LiPo | 8–10 hours of continuous runtime |
| Form Factor | 3mm insole + IP68-rated module | Fits inside standard footwear; safe for daily, all-weather use |
| Sampling Rate | 100 Hz pressure sensing | Clinical-grade temporal resolution |
| Connectivity | BLE 5.0 + companion mobile app | Real-time patient analytics and remote physician monitoring |

### Gait Parameters Tracked

Step length · Stride length · Dorsiflexion · Plantarflexion · Inversion · Eversion · Foot progression angle

### Key Capabilities

- **Pressure Mapping** — High-density sensor array captures dynamic load distribution across the entire footprint
- **Real-Time Gait Analysis** — Monitors pronation, supination, cadence, and strike pattern continuously, not just during a clinic snapshot
- **Preventive Insights** — Flags pressure and gait deviations before they progress to pain, injury, or ulceration
- **Remote Monitoring** — Lets physicians track patient recovery and risk between appointments through a dedicated portal

## Clinical & Research Foundation

SAARTHI/IOLMS is built on a peer-reviewed evidence base rather than a novel, unvalidated approach:

- Continuous plantar pressure monitoring is explicitly recommended by the **IWGDF (International Working Group on the Diabetic Foot) 2023 Guidelines** for high-risk diabetic foot patients.
- A randomised controlled trial (Abbott et al., *Lancet Digital Health*, 2019) found that continuous pressure monitoring with real-time feedback substantially reduced diabetic foot ulcer recurrence compared to standard care.
- Independent research has shown that ambulatory gait monitoring detects clinically significant changes in gait and stride variability weeks before they would be caught at a scheduled clinic visit — relevant to both fall prevention in the elderly and post-surgical recovery tracking.

We are working with **Dr. Vijay G. Goni**, HOD Orthopaedics at **PGIMER Chandigarh**, as a clinical partner and advisor.
